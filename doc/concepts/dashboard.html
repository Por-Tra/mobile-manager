Dashboard

The dashboard measures without judging: the domain qualifies, the interface phrases (“40 min de moins qu’hier”, “Objectif dépassé aujourd’hui : demain est un autre jour”). Days above the goal use a neutral colour, never red.

Calculation

DashboardCalculator.summarize is pure and turns a DashboardInput into a DashboardSummary:

  • Total and comparison with the previous period of the same length (yesterday, or the 7 days before): Trend LESS, MORE, SAME (within 5 minutes) or NO_HISTORY.
  • Apps sorted by time with their share (AppUsage).
  • Last 7 days series for the chart (DayTotal), with the goal line.
  • Zones (ZoneStats): time read from zone_usage (“Reels (Instagram) : 32 min / 50 min aujourd’hui” when a quota exists) and automatic exits (ZONE_REDIRECTED).
  • Interruptions (Interruptions): blocks by reason (BlockKind, read from the journal detail) and zone exits.
  • Goals (Goals): days under the daily goal, days where a daily limit was reached, focus sessions completed or ended early.

Screen time

UsageCalculator.usageByPackage follows the global foreground timeline (one app at a time; opening an app closes the previous one; screen off, shutdown and startup close everything). Mobile Manager, launchers, the system UI and keyboards are excluded from the total (SystemWhitelistProvider.nonScreenTimePackages). Durations are rounded to the minute (Formats.duration), “< 1 min” for very short ones.

Daily snapshots

Android keeps detailed usage events for about a week only. DailyUsageSnapshotter freezes every missing day among the last 14 (yesterday included) into the daily_usage table; a __none__ row marks a day recorded without usage. It runs at app start, when the dashboard opens, and from DailyUsageWorker (periodic WorkManager job every 24 h, battery not low). The current day is always computed live. Loading: DashboardLoader.

Goal

An optional daily screen time goal is stored in DataStore (screen_time_goal_min). The home screen shows a “Today” card and the top bar has a dashboard icon. Changing the goal requires the trusted person code when one is set.
