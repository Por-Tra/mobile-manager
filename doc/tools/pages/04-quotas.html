Daily quotas

A quota is a cumulative daily duration — for example 50 minutes of Instagram Reels. Once it is used, the zone is closed until midnight: no pause screen, no extra time. Messages and the rest of the app stay available.

A quota is a limit rule

No new concept was added to the engine: a quota is a {{RuleAction.Limit}} on a {{Target.Zone}} (Zone(instagram, reels) Limit 50 min), or on a {{Target.App}} for a whole app (App(tiktok) Limit 30 min). The only difference is what is measured: {{RuleEngine}} compares a zone limit with {{AppRequest.zoneUsageToday}} and an app limit with {{AppRequest.usageToday}}.

Measuring time in a zone

Android does not know about zones, so their time is measured by the app itself:

  • {{ZoneUsageAccumulator}} (domain, pure) adds the time between two consecutive observations “in the zone”, capped at 15 s per gap so that a frozen screen or a suspended service never counts indefinitely, and split at midnight so each day gets its share ({{ZoneUsageDelta}}).
  • {{ZoneEnforcer}} observes at least every 5 s while an accessible zone is on screen (a playing video produces no event) and exactly when the remaining quota runs out.
  • Time is credited before the decision, so the quota is judged on up-to-date usage.
  • {{ZoneUsageRecorder}} buffers the deltas in memory and writes them to the zone_usage table every 15 s of counted time and when the app leaves the foreground or the screen turns off. {{ZoneUsageRecorder.usageToday}} adds what is stored and what is still buffered.

When the quota is reached

  1. {{EnforcementCoordinator.evaluateZone}} returns {{Decision.Blocked}} with {{BlockReason.DailyLimitReached}}.
  2. The zone is left (exit actions), its button is masked, and a short message says “Quota de Reels atteint pour aujourd’hui (50 min)”.
  3. Nothing else happens until midnight; the next day starts from zero.

Settings

Settings › “Zones et quotas” ({{ZoneRulesViewModel}}) lists every zone known by the detectors with three choices — Blocked, Quota (5 min to 3 h; 1 and 2 min in debug builds) or Allowed — and the time used today (“32 min sur 50 min aujourd’hui”). Whole-app limits are set in the default rules editor. Every change requires the trusted person code when one is set. A profile can still allow a zone explicitly (Rule(Zone, Allow)).

History: anti-doomscrolling

Earlier versions had pause screens after a continuous session and cooldown blocks. Field use showed they were easy to dismiss and annoying, so they were replaced by quotas: pauses, continuous sessions, the scroll_guard table (dropped in schema 6) and its preferences are gone. Old event types stay in {{EventType}} so the journal can still be read, and old session_limit blocks are counted as rule blocks by the dashboard.
