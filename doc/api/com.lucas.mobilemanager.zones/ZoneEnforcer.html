ZoneEnforcer class

Package: com.lucas.mobilemanager.zonesModule: :appSource: ZoneEnforcer.kt

Automatically leaves zones that are blocked or whose quota is reached (Reels, Shorts…) in an allowed app, masks their entry points and measures the time spent in allowed zones. Reads the structure of the screen (identifiers, tab labels, selected state) of the apps that have detectors only; nothing is recorded.
