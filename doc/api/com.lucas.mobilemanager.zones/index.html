EntryPointMaskclassMasks the entry points of an inaccessible zone (Reels button, Shorts tab): a TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY window (no extra permission) placed exactly over the element, with the colour of the tab bar, that swallows taps.

ZoneEnforcerclassAutomatically leaves zones that are blocked or whose quota is reached (Reels, Shorts…) in an allowed app, masks their entry points and measures the time spent in allowed zones.
ZoneUsageRecorderclassTime spent in zones, for quotas.
