EntryPointMask class

Package: com.lucas.mobilemanager.zonesModule: :appSource: EntryPointMask.kt

Masks the entry points of an inaccessible zone (Reels button, Shorts tab): a TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY window (no extra permission) placed exactly over the element, with the colour of the tab bar, that swallows taps. The app itself is not modified.
